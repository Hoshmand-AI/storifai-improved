How it works

A state-of-the-art pipeline using CLIP and cross-image attention to understand your photos.

Step 01
Upload your photos
Select 3–5 photos from any event — travel, family, everyday moments.
Step 02
CLIP vision encoding
OpenAI's CLIP model encodes each photo into rich semantic embeddings — understanding objects, scenes, and emotions.
Step 03
Cross-image attention
A custom attention layer connects your photos together, so the story understands how scenes relate across time.
Step 04
Transformer story generation
A Transformer decoder generates three uniquely toned narratives — warm, vivid, and reflective — so you can pick the best.

About this model

The improved Storifai model — built for CS 747 Deep Learning at George Mason University.

This is the improved version of Storifai, replacing ResNet-50 with CLIP ViT-B/32 for richer image understanding and swapping the LSTM decoder for a Transformer decoder with cross-image attention. Trained on 40,155 VIST stories. The improved model generates three differently-toned stories per upload so you can choose the one that fits your memory best.
